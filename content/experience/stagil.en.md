My time at STAGIL GmbH marked an important turning point in my career as I specialized in the Atlassian ecosystem. In this role, I was able to combine my technical skills with consulting expertise and became a trusted point of contact for our customers.

During my nearly three years at STAGIL, I accompanied numerous complex Atlassian projects from conception to successful implementation. Particularly challenging and instructive were the cloud migration projects, where I supported customers in transferring their existing server and data center installations to the Atlassian Cloud. These projects required not only deep technical understanding but also the ability to accompany change management processes.

A central aspect of my work was the continuous support of Atlassian customer environments. I was responsible for deployments, updates, maintenance, as well as planning and implementation of system changes. Through proactive monitoring and preventive measures, I was able to continuously improve the availability and performance of the managed systems.

As a technical consultant, I developed customized solutions for customers' specific challenges. My strength was in analyzing complex technical requirements and translating them into practical solution architectures. 2nd-level support rounded out my range of responsibilities and enabled me to gain deep insights into various technology stacks.

**Focus Areas:**
- Atlassian System Administration (Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket)
- Cloud Migration and Transformation Projects
- Requirements Analysis and Solution Architecture
- Change Management and Customer Consulting
- Proactive System Maintenance and Performance Optimization
- Technical Support and Troubleshooting
