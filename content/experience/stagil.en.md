After five years as a system engineer at T-Systems MMS, I sought a new challenge that would combine my technical expertise with consulting competence. At STAGIL GmbH, I specialized in the Atlassian ecosystem and evolved from an implementer to a consultant who advises clients not only technically but also strategically.

During my almost three years at STAGIL, I guided numerous complex Atlassian projects from conception to successful implementation. Particularly challenging and educational were the cloud migration projects, where I supported clients in transitioning their existing server and data center installations to the Atlassian cloud. These projects required not only deep technical understanding but also the ability to accompany change management processes and guide stakeholders through transformations.

A central aspect of my work was the continuous support of Atlassian customer environments. I was responsible for deployments, updates, maintenance, as well as planning and implementing system changes. Through proactive monitoring and preventive measures, I continuously improved the availability and performance of the supported systems. This phase was my school of customer consulting: Here I learned to present technical solutions so that decision-makers immediately recognize the added value.

As a technical consultant, I developed customized solutions for clients' specific challenges. My strength lay in analyzing complex technical requirements and translating them into practical solution architectures. Second-level support rounded out my task spectrum and provided me with deep insights into various technology stacks.

This position was my springboard from pure system engineer to business consultant. I learned not just to build systems, but to understand business processes and align IT solutions with them. This ability shapes my work to this day.

**Focus Areas:**
- Atlassian System Administration (Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket)
- Cloud Migration and Transformation Projects
- Requirements Analysis and Solution Architecture
- Change Management and Customer Consulting
- Proactive System Maintenance and Performance Optimization
- Technical Support and Troubleshooting
